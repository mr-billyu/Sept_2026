God Within Me Prayer 1

Since Baptism,
God Himself has come to dwell within me.

In joy and in sorrow,
in light and in darkness,
He remains within me.

By day and by night,
in labor and in rest,
His presence abides in me.

Let me not forget,
let me not neglect
this God within me.

I need only turn inward
to be with my God who dwells in me.

He longs to hear my voice,
He delights when I remember Him.

My greatest treasure---
the living God within me.

Only sin, freely chosen,
can drive this God from me.

Oh what desolation that would be,
living without God within me.

But don't despair,
the Sacrament of Reconciliation
can bring God within you back.

Oh what a loving God we have.

(Inspired by 1 Corinthians 3:16,John 14:23 and Luke 15:11-32; in the spirit of St. Elizabeth of the Trinity, who prayed, "Let me live my life as one continuous communion with the God who is within me.")

God Within Me Prayer 2

Lord, I believe You are here.
Not far away, not hidden in the clouds ---
but here within me.

You are the life of my soul,
the light that burns quietly beneath my thoughts,
the peace that waits within me.

I turn inward now,
not to find myself,
but to find You, living in me.

Jesus, You dwell in my heart through grace.
You have made my soul Your home.
Let me rest in Your presence ---
not speaking much, just being with You.

(Pause here for silent awareness of simply "being with Him.")

Lord, drive out all that is not of You.
Cleanse the shadows, heal the wounds,
until my heart is a clear dwelling for Your light.

Remain with me,
and help me remain with You ---
in this moment, and through the day ahead.

Amen.
